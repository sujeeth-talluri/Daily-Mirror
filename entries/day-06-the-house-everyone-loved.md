A young couple found a beautiful house.

It had everything they had dreamed of.

Excited, they shared the photos with their family and close friends.

"Wow! This is beautiful."

"You should book it before someone else does."

"Don't miss this opportunity."

Almost everyone had the same opinion.

Only one friend said something different.

"It does look nice," he said. "But before you decide, go and see it once after a heavy rain."

The couple laughed.

"What difference will that make?"

"I don't know," the friend replied. "Maybe nothing. But if I were buying it, I'd want to know."

A week later, after a night of heavy rain, they visited again.

The basement was flooded.

Water had seeped into the walls.

The house looked exactly the same from the outside.

But now they knew something they couldn't have seen before.

On the drive home, the husband smiled and said,

"I'm really glad we had one friend who cared more about protecting us than pleasing us."

That got me thinking...

We all enjoy people who agree with us.

But some of the biggest mistakes in life could be avoided if we learned to value the friend who is willing to ask the uncomfortable question.

Not because they want to discourage us.

But because they genuinely care about us.

Just a thought...

Do I value the friends who make me feel good... or the ones who help me make better decisions?
