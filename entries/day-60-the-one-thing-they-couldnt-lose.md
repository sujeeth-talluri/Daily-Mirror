A few days ago, I watched a movie based on the India–Pakistan Partition.

As families were forced to leave their homes overnight, I couldn't stop asking myself one question.

What would I have done if I had been there?

Imagine locking the front door of your house, knowing you'll probably never return.

Walking away from everything your family had built over generations.

Taking only what your hands could carry.

No certainty about tomorrow.

No idea where the next meal would come from.

No guarantee that life would ever feel normal again.

History has repeated this story many times.

Different countries.

Different generations.

Different names.

But always the same heartbreak.

People leaving everything behind...

and beginning life again in a place they never imagined.

As I watched those stories unfold, one thought stayed with me.

It wasn't money that kept them moving.

It wasn't certainty.

It wasn't comfort.

It was hope.

Hope that tomorrow could still be different from today.

Hope that what was lost wasn't the end of their story.

Hope is a strange thing.

It's like breath.

As long as we keep breathing, our body keeps fighting to live.

As long as hope keeps breathing, our soul keeps finding a reason to move forward.

🪞 The Mirror

Looking into my own mirror...

I realised something.

Most of us will never be forced to leave our country.

But many of us quietly abandon hope long before we ever lose everything.

A medical report.

A broken relationship.

A failed business.

A difficult marriage.

A dream that never happened.

A prayer that seems unanswered.

Slowly...

without even noticing...

we begin telling ourselves,

"This is how my life will always be."

And that's how hope quietly stops breathing.

History has shown us that people can survive almost anything.

They can survive losing a home.

Losing a country.

Losing everything they own.

But they cannot keep moving once they become convinced that tomorrow has nothing left to offer.

Hope doesn't remove today's pain.

It simply refuses to let today's pain decide tomorrow's story.

💭 Think about it...

Where in my life have I quietly stopped hoping?

What situation have I already declared impossible...

even though life is still asking me to keep walking?

If hope is the breath of the soul...

have I been protecting it...

or have I slowly allowed it to suffocate?
