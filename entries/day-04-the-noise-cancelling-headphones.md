A young man bought an expensive pair of noise-cancelling headphones.

"They're amazing," he told his grandfather. "I can't hear anything around me."

A few days later, they were walking together through a park.

The grandfather suddenly stopped.

"Do you hear that?" he asked.

"Hear what?"

"The birds."

The young man removed his headphones.

The park was alive with birdsong.

"I never noticed them before," he admitted.

The grandfather smiled.

"The birds were always singing. You were just listening to something else."

That evening, those words stayed with him.

He realised life is much the same.

The biggest opportunities... The best advice... The deepest relationships... Even moments of peace...

are often not absent.

They're simply drowned out by everything else we're listening to.

🪞 The Mirror

Not everything valuable is loud.

Sometimes what changes our life isn't another voice shouting for our attention...

It's finally becoming quiet enough to hear what has been there all along.

💭 Reflection

What quiet voice in my life have I stopped hearing because I've become too accustomed to the noise?
