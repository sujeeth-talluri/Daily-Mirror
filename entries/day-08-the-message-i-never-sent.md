A group of friends noticed that one of them had changed.

He stopped joining their weekend meetups.

He rarely replied to messages.

When he did, it was always,

"I'm okay."

One friend thought,

"He probably needs some space."

Another thought,

"His family must be taking care of him."

A third thought,

"Someone closer to him will check on him."

Weeks passed.

Nobody called.

Nobody visited.

Months later, they met him again.

He smiled and said,

"There were days I kept looking at my phone... hoping one of you would call."

There was a long silence.

One of the friends quietly replied,

"We noticed..."

"We just thought someone else would do something."

For the first time, they realised that doing nothing had been a decision too.

That sentence stayed with me.

Not because anyone wanted to hurt him.

But because they never realised how much their silence could hurt.

That got me thinking...

Some of life's biggest regrets don't come from the wrong things we did.

They come from the right things we knew we should have done... but kept postponing.

A phone call.

A message.

Five minutes of our time.

Sometimes that's all it takes to remind someone they're not alone.

Just a thought...

Is there someone in my life I've been quietly thinking about...

Someone I've sensed might need a call, a message, or simply someone to check on them...

But I've kept telling myself,

"Someone else will do it."

If I were walking through one of the darkest seasons of my life...

Would I want the people around me to think the same?

And finally...

When I sense someone needs a little nudge, what kind of person do I want to be?
