A man stood in a long queue at the railway station.

It moved slowly.

Every few minutes, he took one small step forward.

He checked his watch.

Looked at the people ahead of him.

Sighed.

And waited.

Almost an hour later, he finally reached the counter.

The man behind the glass looked at him and smiled.

"Sir... this is the reservation enquiry counter."

"The ticket booking queue is over there."

The man turned around.

There it was.

Another long queue.

He hadn't wasted an hour because he was impatient.

He had wasted an hour because he had been waiting in the wrong place.

The problem wasn't the waiting.

It was waiting for the wrong thing.

🪞 The Mirror

That made me think.

Life can be like that.

We work hard.

We give our best.

We spend years chasing what we believe will finally satisfy us.

Then one day we get there...

only to realise it never had what our heart was looking for.

There's nothing wrong with building a career.

Owning a home.

Dreaming big.

The question is...

what are we expecting those things to give us?

Some things can make life comfortable.

But they can never satisfy the deepest hunger of the heart.

💭 Think about it...

If all your biggest goals came true tomorrow...

would your heart finally be satisfied?

Or are you patiently waiting...

in the wrong queue?
