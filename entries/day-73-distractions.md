One thing I've noticed about distractions is that they rarely look like distractions.

Most of them look harmless.

Just five minutes on the phone.

Just one more video.

Just one quick conversation.

Just one small interruption.

Before we realize it, five minutes become an hour... and one day quietly becomes weeks.

I experienced that myself recently.

For the first time since starting The Daily Mirror, I stopped writing for twenty-three days.

It wasn't because I decided to stop.

Life simply became busy, and little by little, I drifted away from something that mattered to me.

That made me realise something.

We rarely walk away from what matters in one big decision.

More often, we slowly drift away through a series of small distractions.

🪞 The Mirror

Looking into my own mirror...

We're already in the last quarter of 2026.

Just three months remain.

At the beginning of this year, many of us started with goals we genuinely wanted to achieve.

Books we wanted to read.

Habits we wanted to build.

Health we wanted to improve.

Relationships we wanted to invest in.

Dreams we wanted to pursue.

How many of those goals did we intentionally give up?

Probably very few.

How many quietly slipped away because we kept getting distracted?

Perhaps that's the harder question.

Every time I say yes to something that distracts me...

I'm unknowingly saying no to something that mattered more.

Maybe today isn't about setting new goals.

Maybe it's about returning to the work I quietly walked away from...

and refusing to come down for things that matter less.

💭 Think about it...

What important work have I quietly drifted away from?

What distractions keep pulling me away from it?

What is one decision I can make today...

to stay focused on what truly matters?
