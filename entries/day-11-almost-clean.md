A father asked his son to clean the garage before they left for a family trip.

When the son finished, the garage looked neat.

The floor was swept.

Everything was back in place.

As they were about to leave, the father noticed a small pile of swept garbage pushed behind the door.

The son smiled and said,

"I'll clean that later."

The father looked at him and quietly asked,

"So... is the garage clean?

Or does it just look clean?"

The son had no answer.

That question stayed with me.

Many of us don't ignore everything.

We fix many things.

We improve many things.

But there are a few areas we quietly push into a corner and tell ourselves,

"I'll deal with it later."

Over time, partial obedience starts feeling like complete obedience.

It looks good from the outside.

But deep inside, we know what's still hiding behind the door.

🪞 The Mirror...

If my life were that garage...

What have I pushed into the corner?

What have I been planning to fix "one day"...

even though I already know it needs my attention today?
