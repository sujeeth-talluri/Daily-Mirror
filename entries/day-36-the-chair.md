There was an old wooden chair in the corner of the house.

Every evening, Grandpa would sit in it.

The grandchildren would climb onto his lap.

The family would gather around him.

That chair was never empty.

Years passed.

Grandpa was no more.

The chair remained exactly where it had always been.

One day, while cleaning the house, someone said,

"This old chair is taking up space. Shall we throw it away?"

The room suddenly fell silent.

His granddaughter quickly replied,

"No... please don't."

"Every time I look at that chair... I remember Grandpa."

Nobody had cared much about the chair when he sat in it every day.

Now...

nobody wanted to lose it.

🪞 The Mirror

Funny how we often discover the value of someone...

only after their place becomes empty.

The parent who quietly sacrificed.

The spouse who silently cared.

The friend who was always available.

The colleague who made everyone's work easier.

The neighbour who was always willing to help.

We rarely say,

"I'm glad you're in my life."

We assume there will always be another tomorrow.

Until one day...

there isn't.

💭 Think about it...

Who in your life has become so familiar...

that you've quietly stopped noticing their value?

Don't wait until an empty chair teaches you...

what a full chair could have.
