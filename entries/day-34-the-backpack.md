A father and his son had been looking forward to their mountain trek for weeks.

The first hour was beautiful.

The air was cool.

The view kept getting better.

The boy couldn't stop smiling.

But as the climb became steeper, his excitement slowly faded.

"Dad... my backpack is getting too heavy."

His father smiled.

"Let's have a look."

Inside were empty water bottles.

A lunch box they had already finished.

A broken torch.

A few smooth stones the boy had picked up because they looked nice.

One by one, the father placed them on the ground.

Then he handed the backpack back.

The boy wore it again.

His face lit up.

"It's so much lighter!"

The father smiled and said,

"The mountain didn't become easier."

"You simply stopped carrying things that were no longer helping you climb."

The boy looked at the pile beside the trail.

Then quietly asked,

"Dad... when did my bag become this heavy?"

His father gently replied,

"One small thing at a time."

🪞 Mirror

That conversation stayed with me.

Maybe that's how life becomes heavy too.

Rarely because of one big event.

Usually because of little things we never put down.

A hurt from a relationship that ended years ago...

yet still makes us afraid to trust someone who has done nothing to deserve that fear.

A failure that quietly became our identity.

A belief someone spoke over us years ago...

and we've been carrying it as though it were the truth.

Even ideas about God that we've never stopped to question, though they have only made us fear Him instead of knowing Him.

None of these arrive looking heavy.

They become heavy because we keep carrying them.

And after a while...

they don't just weigh us down.

They begin to shape the way we see every person, every opportunity, every relationship... even God Himself.

Makes me think.

What am I still carrying today... that is not only making my journey harder, but also keeping me from seeing the truth that God has been trying to show me all along?
