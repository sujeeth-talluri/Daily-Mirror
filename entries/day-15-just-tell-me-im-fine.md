A man went for his yearly health check.

A few days later, the doctor called him.

"Your reports don't look good. If you don't make some changes now, this could become serious."

He didn't like what he heard.

He went to another doctor.

The second doctor said the same thing.

He went to a third.

Then a fourth.

He wasn't looking for treatment.

He was looking for someone who would say,

"You're perfectly fine."

Months later, his condition became much worse.

One of his friends quietly asked,

"Did four doctors get it wrong... or was there something you didn't want to hear?"

There was no answer.

🪞 The Mirror...

Most of us would probably shake our heads at that man.

"Why didn't he simply listen?"

But maybe we do the same thing in other areas of life.

Sometimes a spouse points out something in us.

A close friend does.

A parent.

A manager.

Someone who genuinely wants the best for us.

Instead of stopping and thinking,

we explain...

we defend...

or we simply stop listening.

The real danger isn't that nobody tells me the truth.

The real danger is reaching a place where I only want to hear what makes me feel comfortable.
