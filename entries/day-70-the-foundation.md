Whenever we admire a beautiful building, our eyes naturally go to what stands above the ground.

The height.

The design.

The architecture.

Very few of us stop to think about what lies beneath it.

Yet every engineer knows that a building is only as strong as its foundation.

That made me think about life.

No successful life is ever truly self-made.

Every one of us is standing on a foundation we didn't build ourselves.

Parents who sacrificed.

Teachers who shaped us.

Mentors who guided us.

Managers who trusted us.

Friends who encouraged us.

Long before people noticed the building...

someone was quietly laying the foundation.

🪞 The Mirror

Looking into my own mirror...

I wonder if I've become so focused on the building...

that I've forgotten the foundation.

Every achievement in my life carries fingerprints that aren't mine alone.

Someone opened a door.

Someone corrected me.

Someone believed in me before I believed in myself.

Hard work matters.

But hard work is rarely the whole story.

Perhaps one of the quietest forms of pride isn't saying,

"I did this all by myself."

It's simply forgetting the people who helped us become who we are.

Maybe gratitude isn't only remembering our blessings.

Maybe it's remembering the people God used to bring those blessings into our lives.

💭 Think about it...

Who laid the foundation that I'm standing on today?

Whose influence have I quietly stopped acknowledging?

When I tell the story of my life...

do I remember those who helped build it?
