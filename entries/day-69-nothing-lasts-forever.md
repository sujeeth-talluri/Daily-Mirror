Whenever I read history books or watch documentaries about ancient kingdoms, one thing always fascinates me.

Some of those kingdoms once ruled the world.

They had powerful armies.

Thriving economies.

Magnificent cities.

To the people living in those days, they probably looked invincible.

Recently, I was reading about the ancient kingdom of Tyre.

It had everything a nation could hope for.

Wealth.

Trade.

Influence.

Security.

Looking at it from that point in history, no one would have imagined that its greatness would one day become only a chapter in history.

That made me stop and think.

History keeps telling the same story.

Things that once looked permanent...

rarely stay that way forever.

🪞 The Mirror

Looking into my own mirror...

I wonder how many temporary things I've quietly started treating as permanent.

A career.

A home.

Financial security.

Good health.

A position.

A reputation.

None of them are wrong.

But none of them were ever promised to last forever.

Perhaps that's why history is such a good teacher.

It quietly reminds us that every generation believed their world would remain exactly as it was.

Every generation eventually discovered otherwise.

Maybe that's why we should hold every blessing with gratitude...

and every possession with humility.

Not because we expect to lose them...

but because they were never meant to become the foundation of our confidence.

Perhaps the real question isn't,

"Will things change?"

History has already answered that.

Perhaps the better question is,

"What am I holding on to as though it will last forever?"

💭 Think about it...

What have I quietly started treating as permanent...

even though history repeatedly reminds me otherwise?

If everything around me changed tomorrow...

what would still remain?

Am I building my confidence on what is temporary...

or on what truly lasts?
