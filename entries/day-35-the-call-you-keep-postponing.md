A young man stared at his phone.

His thumb hovered over one name.

"Dad."

He sighed... locked the phone... and slipped it back into his pocket.

The business wasn't doing well.

Bills were piling up.

He had spent weeks trying to solve everything by himself.

"I'll call once I have some good news," he told himself.

Days turned into weeks.

Nothing changed.

Finally, one evening, he made the call.

His father answered on the very first ring.

Before the son could explain anything, his father quietly asked,

"What took you so long?"

The son replied,

"I wanted to fix it before I came to you."

There was a long silence.

Then his father said,

"My son... if you could fix it yourself, you wouldn't have needed to call me."

🪞 The Mirror

How often do we postpone the very conversation we need the most?

Not because the door is closed...

but because we want to arrive with better news.

We tell ourselves,

"I'll pray after I overcome this."

"I'll come back once I've changed."

"I'll seek God after I've sorted my life out."

Yet the greatest barrier between us and God is rarely our problem.

It's our pride that whispers,

"Handle it first... then go."

💭 Think about it...

What have you been trying to fix before bringing it to God?

Maybe He was never waiting for your solution.

Maybe He was simply waiting for your call.
