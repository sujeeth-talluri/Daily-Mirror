Think about it.

Your phone buzzes.

A close friend sends a message.

"I have some great news... I got promoted!"

You immediately reply,

"Wow! Congratulations! So happy for you. You deserve it."

You genuinely mean what you typed.

But after putting your phone aside...

your joy quietly fades.

A few thoughts begin to creep in.

"I've worked just as hard."

"Why does this happen to everyone except me?"

"When will it be my turn?"

You brush those thoughts aside and continue with your day.

Months pass.

Your phone buzzes again.

The same friend.

"I lost my job today."

Your heart sinks.

You reply,

"I'm really sorry. Don't worry. You'll come back stronger."

You genuinely mean those words too.

But somewhere deep inside...

another thought quietly whispers,

"Life has a way of balancing things."

"At least now... we're on the same level."

You immediately hate that thought.

Because that's not who you believe you are.

That night, lying on your bed, one question refuses to leave your mind.

When did I become this person?

Not the person who celebrates another person's success...

but the person whose own peace is disturbed by it.

Not the person who grieves over another person's pain...

but the person who somehow feels lighter because of it.

🪞 The Mirror...

One thing I've observed in life is this.

Very few people are happy when others fail.

Most of us are simply relieved.

Relieved that someone who was ahead of us... isn't anymore.

And that realization should make us pause.

Because if someone else's success steals my joy...

and someone else's failure gives it back...

then the real problem isn't their success or their failure.

The real problem is my heart.

Today, don't think about the people around you.

Think about yourself.

Can I genuinely celebrate when someone else goes ahead of me?

Can I genuinely grieve when someone else falls behind me?

Or has comparison quietly changed me into someone I never intended to become?

Because the saddest day in life is not the day someone else falls.

It's the day their fall becomes the reason for my peace.
