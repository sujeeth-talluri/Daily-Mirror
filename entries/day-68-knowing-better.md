We live in a generation where knowledge has never been more accessible.

Yet despite knowing so much...

many of us keep repeating the same mistakes.

That made me think.

Perhaps experience, by itself, doesn't make a person wiser.

Learning from experience does.

I've completed six full marathons so far.

Experience has already taught me what kind of training is needed to run the marathon time I'm aiming for.

But knowing the training plan has never made me a better runner.

Every marathon reminds me of the same lesson.

If I neglect my long runs...

if I ignore my strength training...

if I lose consistency...

I'll most likely hit the wall around the 30-kilometre mark.

The experience is there.

The knowledge is there.

The question is...

Have I learned from it?

🪞 The Mirror

Looking into my own mirror...

I realised the biggest gap in my life isn't between ignorance and knowledge.

It's between knowledge...

and action.

Most of the time, I already know what needs to change.

The difficult part isn't learning it.

It's doing it.

Perhaps that's why we keep repeating the same struggles.

Not because life hasn't taught us.

But because we've convinced ourselves that knowing is enough.

Experience gives us lessons.

Wisdom is choosing to live by them.

Maybe the question isn't,

"What more do I need to know?"

Maybe it's,

"What do I already know... that I've still not acted upon?"

💭 Think about it...

What lesson has life already taught me...

that I keep refusing to learn?

Where have I confused knowing with doing?

Am I waiting for another painful reminder...

before acting on what I already know is right?
