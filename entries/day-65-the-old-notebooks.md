A few days ago, I came across some old notebooks that my wife and I had written about a decade ago.

As I turned those pages, I found monthly budgets, EMI calculations, salary goals, and dreams we hoped would one day become reality.

Reading them took me back to a very different season of life.

After returning from the United States and settling back in India, paying the home loan for our house on an Indian salary felt almost impossible. There were days when I genuinely believed the only way I could clear that loan was by somehow going back to the US.

Fast forward to today.

The home loan has been completely paid off.

Not by going back to the US.

Not through one extraordinary breakthrough.

But through years of God's faithfulness, one ordinary month at a time.

As I closed those notebooks, one thought stayed with me.

Without those pages, I might have forgotten how impossible today's ordinary life once seemed.

🪞 The Mirror

Looking into my own mirror...

I wonder how many blessings have quietly become ordinary.

How many things that I now expect...

were once things I desperately prayed for.

The job.

The home.

The family.

The opportunities.

The stability.

Perhaps that's one of life's quiet dangers.

Not that we lose God's blessings...

but that we slowly lose the memory of His grace.

Yesterday's prayer has a way of becoming today's expectation.

Yesterday's miracle quietly becomes today's routine.

Sometimes all it takes is an old notebook...

an old photograph...

or a forgotten prayer journal...

to remind us how faithfully God has led us.

Those old notebooks don't simply remind me where my journey began.

They remind me that every blessing I enjoy today has a story behind it.

A story of waiting.

A story of uncertainty.

A story of prayers.

A story of grace.

Perhaps remembering where we've come from...

is one of God's ways of keeping us humble.

💭 Think about it...

What in my life has become so ordinary...

that I've forgotten it was once an answer to prayer?

What reminds me of where my journey began...

and of how faithfully God has led me this far?

Have today's familiar blessings quietly made me forget yesterday's unforgettable grace?
