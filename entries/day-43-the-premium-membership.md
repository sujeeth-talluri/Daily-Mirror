I know quite a few people who proudly say,

"I have a premium gym membership."

It gives them access to one of the best fitness centres in the city.

A fully equipped gym.

Swimming pool.

Indoor games.

Everything needed to become fit.

Whenever fitness comes up...

they happily talk about their membership.

One day, a friend smiled and said,

"I go to the gym almost every day."

Then he laughed and added,

"Most days, I just meet my friends there, have a coffee, talk for a while, scroll through my phone... and come back home."

We both laughed.

But that sentence stayed with me.

The membership wasn't the problem.

The gym wasn't the problem.

Even walking into the gym wasn't enough.

Walking into the gym never made anyone fit.

Working out did.

🪞 The Mirror

That made me think.

Life can be like that.

Going to the gym doesn't make me fit.

Working out does.

Going to school doesn't make me knowledgeable.

Learning does.

Going to church doesn't change me.

Living what I hear does.

Going to the temple doesn't change me.

Living what I learn there does.

Just being around something that can change my life...

doesn't change my life.

Real change begins...

when I stop being satisfied with showing up...

and start living what I already know.

💭 Think about it...

Where in my life have I mistaken showing up...

for actually changing?

What truth have I heard again and again...

but still haven't started living?

Am I becoming a better person, or have I simply become good at being present?
