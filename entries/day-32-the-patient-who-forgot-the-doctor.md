A young man met with a terrible accident.

For weeks, he couldn't even sit up by himself.

His family stayed beside him day and night.

Every morning, his doctor came to see him.

Not because he was paid extra.

But because he genuinely cared.

He encouraged him.

Corrected him when he ignored instructions.

And every time he left, he would smile and say,

"Don't give up. You'll walk again."

Slowly...

he did.

The day he finally walked out of the hospital, his family couldn't stop smiling.

Life slowly returned to normal.

He went back to work.

Met friends.

Started travelling again.

The difficult days became a memory.

Months passed.

One evening, his mother casually asked,

"Have you gone to meet the doctor recently?"

He looked surprised.

"No... but why?"

She smiled and quietly said,

"When you couldn't walk, you couldn't stop talking about him."

"Now that you can..."

"...you don't seem to have time for him."

🪞 Mirror

Makes me think.

Do I remember the One who carried me through my difficult days... only until life becomes easy again?
