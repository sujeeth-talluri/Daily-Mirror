That's Not Fair.

MILESTONE VS JOURNEY

Two employees made mistakes.

One was warned.

The other was given another chance. After the meeting, the first employee walked up to the manager.

"Sir... can I ask you something?"
"Sure."
"Why did you give him another chance? I made a mistake too. I never got one. That's not fair."
The manager smiled.
"I know it looks that way."
"But you've seen one mistake."
"I've seen his journey."

The employee didn't say another word.

🪞 The Mirror...

How many times have I looked at a situation and said,

"That's not fair."
The truth is...

I usually know only one part of the story. There are things I don't know. Things I haven't seen. Things I don't understand.

The next time I feel something is unfair...

Maybe I should pause and ask myself,

"Am I seeing just this moment... or do I really know the whole story?"
Sometimes...

the picture is much bigger than what I can see.
