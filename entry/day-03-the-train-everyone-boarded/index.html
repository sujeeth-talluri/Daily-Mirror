The Train Everyone Boarded

PRIDE · COMPARISON · FALSE PEACE

A man rushed into a crowded railway station. He glanced at the departure board, saw people running towards Platform 4, and instinctively ran with them. He squeezed through the crowd, jumped onto the train just as the doors were closing, and finally sat down, exhausted but relieved. An elderly passenger sitting beside him smiled and said,

"You almost missed your train."
"I did!" the young man replied proudly. "I had to run all the way."

The old man nodded and asked one simple question.

"Where are you travelling?"

The young man mentioned his destination. The old man's smile faded.

"My friend... this train doesn't go there."

Silence filled the space between them.

The young man had done everything right...
He ran hard.
He stayed focused.

He didn't give up. He caught the train.

The only problem was...

He never stopped to check if it was the right one.

🪞 The Mirror

The world celebrates speed, ambition, and achievement.

But before asking, "How fast am I moving?", perhaps the more important question is, "Where am I heading?"

Because success on the wrong path is still a loss.

💭 Reflection

Today, what am I chasing simply because everyone else is running toward it... and have I paused long enough to ask if it's taking me where I truly want to go?
