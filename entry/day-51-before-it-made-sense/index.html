Before It Made Sense

SELF-RELIANCE · TRUST

Recently, I watched a scene from a series based on fighter pilots. One training session caught my attention. The instructor tied five motorcycles together with ropes and asked five trainee pilots to ride them as one single unit. The trainees looked at each other in confusion. What did riding motorcycles have to do with flying fighter jets?

It looked strange.
It looked unnecessary.

It even looked foolish. Yet they trusted their instructor.

So they stopped asking questions...
and started practising.

Months later, during a critical mission, the usual methods no longer worked. In that moment, the same strange exercise became the very thing that helped them work together and accomplish what seemed impossible. Only then did they understand. The instructor had never been preparing them for that exercise. He had been preparing them for a future they couldn't yet see.

🪞 The Mirror

That scene stayed with me. I've realised I don't usually struggle to obey when everything makes sense. The real struggle begins when I don't understand the purpose. I want explanations before obedience. I want to see tomorrow before I take today's step. But life doesn't always work that way. Sometimes the purpose is revealed only after the step is taken.

Maybe I don't have to see what lies ahead...
if I already trust the One who does.

Looking back, some of the most meaningful lessons in life only made sense much later. The understanding came later. The obedience had to come first.

💭 Think about it...

What am I resisting today...
simply because I don't yet understand why?

Am I waiting for every answer before I take the next step...

or am I willing to trust the One who already sees what I cannot?
