The Notice

IGNORING WARNING SIGNS · URGENCY VS TRUTH

A few days ago, Hyderabad witnessed the collapse of a five-storey building. As people discussed the incident, it reminded me of the many demolitions that have taken place across the city over the last couple of years. Many of those buildings had one thing in common. They didn't disappear overnight. A notice came first. Some owners accepted the notice, packed their belongings, and moved on. Others looked at the same notice and carried on with life as usual.

Days passed.
Weeks passed.
Nothing happened.

The building was still standing. Perhaps that's what gave them confidence.

"Maybe nothing will happen after all."
Then one morning...
the machines arrived.

The building didn't come down because the notice was issued that day. It came down because the notice had been ignored all along.

🪞 The Mirror

Looking into my own mirror...

I wonder how often I've done the same.

Not because I never received the notice...
but because nothing happened immediately after it.

Time has a strange way of changing how seriously we take things.

When consequences don't arrive quickly...
we quietly begin believing they never will.

Delay slowly becomes permission. Silence begins to feel like approval.

And before we know it...
we're building our confidence on the fact that nothing has happened yet.

Perhaps the safest decisions in life aren't made when consequences arrive. They're made while there's still time to respond.

💭 Think about it...

Where in my life have I mistaken delay...
for cancellation?

Have I become comfortable...
simply because nothing has happened yet?

What notice have I quietly stopped taking seriously...

just because time has passed?
