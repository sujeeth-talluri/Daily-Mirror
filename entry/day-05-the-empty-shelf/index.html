The Empty Shelf

priderefusal-to-changefalse-peacegratitude

A successful businessman invited an old school teacher to his home.

As they walked through the house, he proudly showed him everything he had built over the years.

The teacher admired it all.

Then he noticed one empty shelf in the study.

"What was meant to go there?" he asked.

The businessman smiled sadly.

"A trophy I never won."

For a moment, the teacher said nothing.

Then he quietly replied,

"Strange, isn't it?"

"Out of an entire house filled with blessings... your eyes still return to the one empty shelf."

The businessman looked around the room.

For the first time, he realised he had spent years enjoying everything he had less and longing for the one thing he didn't.

🪞 The Mirror

It's rarely the absence of something that steals our peace.

More often, it's allowing one unmet desire to become bigger than every blessing already in our hands.

Gratitude doesn't ignore what's missing.

It simply refuses to let it define our life.

💭 Reflection

What's the 'empty shelf' in my life... and has it quietly become more important than everything that's already on the shelves around it?

If this spoke to you, someone else might need it today.
