The Teacher Nobody Remembered

self-examination

At a school reunion, everyone was busy catching up.

Some spoke about their promotions.

Some about their businesses.

Others shared stories about their families.

In one corner of the hall, an old retired teacher was quietly having a cup of tea.

Most people smiled at him as they walked past.

After a while, a man in his forties walked up to him, bent down respectfully and said,

"Sir... I don't know if you remember me."

The teacher smiled.

"I'm sorry... help me."

The man replied,

"When I was in Class 10, I had decided to quit school. You called me aside one afternoon and said, 'I think you're giving up too early. I believe you can do much more than you think.'"

"I don't remember much from that year."

"But I've never forgotten those words."

The teacher looked at him and smiled.

"I'm glad they helped."

The man smiled back.

"Sir... today I perform heart surgeries."

There was a brief silence.

Then he added,

"But years ago, you repaired something much more important... my confidence."

The teacher's eyes filled with tears.

Softly, he said,

"I don't even remember saying those words."

The surgeon replied,

"I never forgot them."

That got me thinking...

There are conversations we forget because they were ordinary to us.

And then there are conversations we never forget because they quietly changed the direction of our lives.

Sometimes, a few words spoken at the right time stay with us for years.

Just a thought...

Can I remember one conversation that changed my life?

Have I ever thanked that person...

Or have I quietly moved on, thinking they were "just words," when for me, they became so much more?
