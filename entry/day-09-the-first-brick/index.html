The First Brick

postponement

A young man dreamed of building a home for his family.

Every year he talked about it.

"I'll start when I have more savings."

"I'll start when my salary increases."

"I'll start when the timing feels right."

Years went by.

One evening, he met an old mason who had built houses all his life.

The old man listened quietly and then asked,

"So... have you laid the first brick?"

The young man smiled.

"Not yet."

The mason picked up a brick lying nearby, placed it on the ground and said,

"Every house I've ever built started exactly like this."

"One brick."

"You don't build a home by waiting until you can see the whole house."

"You build it by laying the first brick."

Those words stayed with him.

The next week, he bought his first load of bricks.

The house wasn't finished that day.

But for the first time...

It had begun.

That got me thinking...

Sometimes we're waiting for confidence before we begin.

Sometimes we're waiting for certainty before we obey.

Sometimes we're waiting for life to become easier before we take the next step.

But maybe progress doesn't begin when everything is clear.

Maybe it begins the moment we decide to lay the first brick.

Just a thought...

Is there an area of my life where I've been waiting for the perfect time...

when all that's really needed is the courage to take the first faithful step?

Maybe the life I'm hoping for doesn't begin with a miracle...

Maybe it begins with one simple act of faith today.
