Just a Little Further

PREMATURE SURRENDER · SELF-DECEPTION

Recently, I spent around five days at a hospital taking care of my father-in-law. The hospital's ground-floor canteen was almost always crowded. Patients' attendants, visitors, hospital staff... everyone seemed to gather there, especially during meal times. Meanwhile, boards on almost every floor pointed to another canteen on the first floor, near Gate 16, open 24 hours. I kept seeing those boards, but still went downstairs. One day, I asked my sister-in-law about the other canteen. She told me there wasn't one. She had actually gone to Gate 16, walked inside, looked around, and returned without finding anything. The next day, I decided to check for myself.

I reached Gate 16, walked inside, continued a little further, took a left turn...

And there it was. A fully functioning canteen. When I told my sister-in-law, she was genuinely surprised. She had looked for it. She just hadn't gone far enough. And I could understand why. From where she had stopped, there was nothing to suggest a canteen was waiting just around the corner. The interesting thing was that neither of us had seen it before.

But somewhere along the way, "I couldn't find it" had become "It isn't there."

🪞 The Mirror

Looking into my own mirror...

I began wondering how many times I've done something similar. Met someone briefly and decided what kind of person they were. Tried something once and concluded it wouldn't work. Heard someone else's experience and accepted their conclusion without exploring it myself. And the strange thing is, I may not even realise I'm making an assumption. Because in my mind, I've already seen enough. Perhaps some of my strongest opinions have been formed from things I never fully explored.

💭 Think about it...

Which of my conclusions are based on what I truly know... and which are based only on how far I've looked?
