Day 44

The Tennis Racket

complacencyself-examination

Back in 2009, I joined a tennis academy.

I was excited.

I bought a good tennis racket. Back then, it was one of the most expensive things I had ever bought for myself.

Every morning, I would wake up early, go for coaching, play for a couple of hours, come back home, get ready, and then leave for the office.

For a few months, that racket did exactly what it was made for.

Then life changed.

I moved to the U.S.

The racket stayed back at my parents' home.

They hung it neatly on the wall.

Years passed.

Every time I visited home, I would see it hanging there.

It looked just as good as before.

Recently, I took it off the wall.

For a moment I thought,

"Maybe I should start playing again."

But the moment I held it...

I knew.

The strings had become loose.

The grip had become hard.

It still looked like a tennis racket.

But it was no longer ready for a game.

For years...

it had become a beautiful decoration.

It had stopped serving the purpose for which it was made.

🪞 The Mirror

That made me think.

Life can be like that.

Sometimes I'm so busy protecting what God has given me...

that I stop using it.

My time.

My abilities.

The opportunities He gives me.

Even my life.

I can become so comfortable...

that I slowly forget why He gave them to me in the first place.

A tennis racket wasn't made to hang on a wall.

It was made to be used.

Maybe it's better for a racket to have worn-out strings from years of playing...

than to look brand new because it was never used.

💭 Think about it...

What in my life has slowly become a decoration...

when it was meant to make a difference?

Am I spending my life...

or simply preserving it?

If God picked up my life today, would He find something that has been faithfully used, or something that has only been carefully kept?
