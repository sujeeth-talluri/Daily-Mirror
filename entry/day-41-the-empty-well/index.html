The Empty Well

self-reliancedistraction

A village was blessed with a fresh water stream.

For generations, every family carried water home from it.

The water was clean.

Cool.

And it never stopped flowing.

One young farmer often thought,

"Why should I walk all the way to the stream every day?"

"I'll dig my own well near my house."

People admired his hard work.

For weeks he dug.

Finally...

he found water.

He smiled and said,

"I don't need the stream anymore."

For a while...

everything seemed fine.

Then summer came.

Day by day...

the well became dry.

One morning, he stood looking into the empty well.

An old man happened to walk by.

He looked at the dry well...

then looked towards the stream that was still flowing.

He quietly asked,

"My son..."

"Why are you standing beside an empty well..."

"...when the stream is still flowing?"

The farmer stood there in silence.

He knew the answer.

🪞 The Mirror

That made me think.

How often do I do the same?

God has never stopped being the source of life.

Yet sometimes I quietly walk away...

looking for something easier...

something closer...

something that promises to satisfy me.

For a while...

it seems enough.

Until one day...

I find myself empty again.

The saddest part isn't that the well became dry.

The saddest part is...

the stream never stopped flowing.

I simply stopped going there.

💭 Think about it...

What well have I been digging...

while quietly walking past the stream?

What have I been expecting to satisfy my heart...

that keeps leaving me empty?

God has never stopped being the source.

The real question isn't whether the stream is still flowing.

The real question is...

Why did I stop going to it?
