It's Probably Nothing.

ignoring-warning-signsfalse-peacepostponement

You're in your 30s.

You take reasonably good care of your health.

You exercise.

You play sports.

You tell yourself,

"I'm healthy. I don't really need a health check-up."

One day, you feel a small discomfort in your chest.

"Probably gas. I didn't eat well yesterday."

After a while, it settles down.

You move on.

A couple of weeks later, it happens again.

"I've had this before. It'll be fine."

It settles down again.

A few days later, while playing badminton, you feel unusually breathless.

"Maybe I'm just tired today."

You continue playing.

A few minutes later...

you collapse.

At the hospital, the doctor says,

"Your body didn't suddenly fail today. It has been trying to get your attention for weeks. Those weren't random incidents. They were warning signs."

You don't keep thinking about the heart attack.

You keep thinking about...

the first warning you chose to ignore.

🪞 The Mirror...

Life has its own warning lights too.

A warning light on a car dashboard.

The same health symptom that keeps coming back.

Conversations at home that are no longer the same.

The same feedback from different people.

A friendship slowly drifting apart.

A child who has suddenly stopped sharing things with you.

A loss of peace that you can't explain.

Most of the time, these are not the real problem.

They're messages asking us to pause and pay attention.

The biggest problems in life rarely arrive without first sending smaller messages.

Life rarely shouts first.

It usually whispers.

When life keeps trying to get my attention... am I listening?
