Where Do I Run First?

false-security

Think about it.

When a child gets frightened...

he doesn't stop to think.

He instinctively runs to the person he trusts most.

When someone suddenly loses their job...

one person immediately opens their banking app.

Another starts calling people.

Another shuts the door and doesn't want to talk to anyone.

When a health report doesn't look good...

one person spends the whole night searching the internet.

Another pretends the report doesn't exist.

Another immediately calls someone they trust.

None of them planned their response.

It just happened.

One thing I've observed in life is this.

Difficult situations don't usually create our first response...

they reveal it.

Our first instinct quietly exposes where we believe our security really is.

Easy days can hide that.

Difficult days rarely do.

🪞 The Mirror...

Think about the last time life suddenly shook you.

A disappointing phone call.

A health concern.

A financial setback.

A broken relationship.

What was the very first place you ran to?

Not after you had time to think.

Immediately.

Because many times...

our first instinct reveals more about us than our best intentions ever do.

The question isn't,

"What do I say I trust?"

The question is,

"When life suddenly changes... where do I run first?"

When life suddenly changes... where do I run first?
