I Thought It Would Always Be There

FALSE SECURITY · TRUST

My very first mobile phone was a Nokia. Those days, Nokia wasn't just another phone brand. It was the phone. If someone asked which phone to buy, the answer was almost always the same.

Nokia.

People joked that the phone would survive a fall better than the floor itself.

It was reliable.
It was trusted.
It was everywhere.

Back then, I don't think anyone seriously imagined a day when Nokia would no longer be the name everyone talked about. Yet, that's exactly what happened.

Not overnight.

Not because it wasn't good. History simply moved on. Looking back today, what surprises me isn't that Nokia changed. It's how certain we all were that it never would.

🪞 The Mirror

That made me quietly look into my own life. How many things have I started treating the same way? Things I simply assume will always be there.

My health.
My strength.
My career.

The people I love. The opportunities I have today.

Not because anyone promised they would remain...
but because they've always been there.
Funny, isn't it?

We become so familiar with today's blessings that we quietly stop imagining life without them. Then life reminds us that nothing in this world was ever meant to carry the weight of our confidence forever.

Companies rise.
Companies fall.
Kingdoms rise.
Kingdoms fall.
Seasons change.
People change.
Life changes.

Perhaps the question was never whether these things would change.

Perhaps the real question is...
What have I been building my life upon all along?

💭 Think about it...

What in my life have I quietly started treating as though it will always be there...
simply because it has always been there until now?

If everything around me can change...

what is the one thing I'm building my life upon that never will?
