Day 42

What Smell?

ignoring-warning-signsself-examination

When I was in school, we lived in a town that had a factory nearby.

Every evening, a strong smell filled the air.

During the first few weeks...

it was terrible.

We would close the windows.

Cover our noses.

And wonder,

"How do people live here every day?"

One evening, I asked a man who had lived there for years,

"Doesn't this smell bother you?"

He smiled and said,

"You'll get used to it."

I didn't believe him.

But he was right.

Months passed.

Life went on.

By the time we were getting ready to leave that town...

someone mentioned the smell again.

For a moment, I actually thought,

"What smell?"

The smell never became less.

We simply got used to it.

🪞 The Mirror

That made me think.

Life can be like that.

The first time I do something I know isn't right...

my heart becomes uncomfortable.

My conscience tells me,

"This isn't right."

But if I ignore that voice...

and keep doing the same thing...

something slowly changes.

Not the habit.

Me.

What once made me uncomfortable...

slowly becomes acceptable.

Not because it became right...

but because I became comfortable with it.

💭 Think about it...

When was the last time my heart was disturbed by something...

that today doesn't bother me at all?

What areas of my life was I once not comfortable with...

but have now quietly become acceptable to me?

Did those things really become right, or have I simply become comfortable living with them?
