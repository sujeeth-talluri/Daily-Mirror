The Small Crack

IGNORING WARNING SIGNS · POSTPONEMENT

For decades, a village depended on a large dam. It had survived every rainy season. It looked as strong as ever. One day, during a routine inspection, an engineer noticed a small crack.

"It isn't dangerous today," he told the village leaders.
"But if we repair it now, it will remain a small crack."

A warning board was placed near the dam.

Days passed.
Nothing happened.
Weeks passed.
Nothing happened.
Months passed.

The warning board slowly became part of the scenery. People walked past it every day. Until they stopped noticing it altogether. Then came the monsoon. One night, the dam gave way. Standing beside the broken dam, someone said,

"It collapsed without warning."

The engineer quietly replied,

"No."
"The warning came long before the collapse."
"We simply ignored it."

🪞 The Mirror

That made me think. The crack wasn't the danger. The warning wasn't the danger.

Ignoring them was.

Life has a way of showing me warning signs long before the damage becomes visible. A conversation I keep postponing. A habit I keep excusing. A conviction I quietly stop listening to. God's warnings are not there to scare me. They're there to save me.

The greatest damage often happens...
not because there were no warnings...
but because I stopped treating them with the urgency they deserved.

💭 Think about it...

Is there a small crack in my life today?

What's the warning I already know about...
but I'm choosing to ignore?

Thank God...

He cared enough to show me the warning before the collapse.

The real question is...
Will I keep walking past it...
or will I repair the crack while it's still small?

Maybe today is the day.
