That's Not Me.

ignoring-warning-signs

One thing I've observed in life is this.

Truth rarely comes to us saying,

"I'm here to expose you."

Most of the time, it comes through ordinary people and ordinary situations.

Your spouse says,

"You've stopped listening to me. Even when I'm talking, your mind is somewhere else."

You immediately reply,

"No... you're just expecting too much."

Your closest friend says,

"You've changed. I don't feel I can talk to you like before."

You smile and say,

"Nothing like that. Life is just busy these days."

Your manager says,

"You're technically very good, but people hesitate to disagree with you."

You walk out thinking,

"They don't know the full picture. They think they know me, but they don't."

The doctor looks at your reports and says,

"Your body has been giving you warning signs for quite some time. Please don't ignore them."

You tell yourself,

"I'm still young. Doctors always make something look bigger than it is. I'll be fine."

Think about it.

None of these people were trying to insult you.

None of them woke up wanting to put you down.

They were simply holding up a mirror.

🪞 The Mirror...

Many times, truth comes into our lives through people who genuinely care about us.

The first question that comes to our mind is usually,

"How do I explain myself?"

Maybe the better question is,

"Is there any truth in what they're saying?"

Not every criticism is true.

But not every criticism is false either.

The day I stop defending myself long enough to honestly examine myself...

is the day I begin to grow.

Because...

Truth exposes.

My response decides who I become.
