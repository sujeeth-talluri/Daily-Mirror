Think About It...

FALSE SECURITY · POSTPONEMENT

Think about it.

A husband hurts his wife during an argument. He knows he messed up. The next day, he buys her flowers. A few weeks later, it happens again. This time, an expensive dinner.

Then jewellery.

The gifts keep changing.

But the words that hurt her...
never do.
Think about it.

Parents work day and night for their daughter.

The best school.
The best clothes.
The best vacations.
The best phone.

One day, their daughter quietly says,

"I know you're doing all this because you love me... but I just wish you had more time for me."
Think about it.

A manager appreciates his team in every town hall. He celebrates every success. Rewards the best performers. But inside the meeting room, nobody feels comfortable telling him,

"I think you're wrong."

One thing I've observed in life is this.

Many times, when changing is difficult...

we become busy doing other good things to compensate.

Instead of rebuilding trust...
we buy gifts.
Instead of giving our time...

we spend more money.

Instead of changing our attitude...

we increase our activities. The good things we do are still good.

The problem is...

they quietly become a replacement for the one thing that actually needs to change.

🪞 The Mirror...

Think about my own life for a moment. Am I also doing this somewhere?

Am I becoming busy doing many good things...
because changing one part of me feels much harder?

Maybe the question isn't,

"What more should I do?"

Maybe the better question is,

"What in me needs to change?"
Because...

Many times, doing another good thing is easier than changing the one thing that's hurting the people I love.
