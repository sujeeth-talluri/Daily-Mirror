The Door I Didn't Force Open

TRUST · PRESENCE

For years, there was one prayer I desperately wanted God to answer.

He never did.

Many years ago, I returned to India after my visa reached its limit in the United States. For a long time, I couldn't make peace with that. My heart was always somewhere else. I kept looking for ways to go back. Around me, I watched others find ways to force those doors open. More than once, I was tempted to do the same. At the time, it looked like they had made the right decision. Years later, some of those very people quietly admitted something they never expected.

The door they fought so hard to open...
cost them far more than they imagined.

Looking back today, I realise something. The greatest blessing in my life wasn't another door opening. It was one particular door remaining closed.

🪞 The Mirror

Looking into my own mirror...

I realised my biggest struggle was never about a country. It was about believing that my peace was waiting behind one particular door.

As long as it stayed closed...

I couldn't enjoy the life already in front of me. Then one day, something changed.

Not my circumstances.
Me.

I didn't find peace because my prayer was answered. I found peace the day I stopped fighting reality.

Looking back now...
I'm grateful not only for the doors God opened...
but also for the one He lovingly never allowed me to force open.

💭 Think about it...

What door have I convinced myself must open...
before I can finally have peace?

How much of my present have I quietly sacrificed...
waiting for a future I've already decided is better?

If that door never opens...
will I keep fighting reality...

or will I learn to embrace the place where I already stand?
