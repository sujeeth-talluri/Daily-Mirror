The Potter's Hands

self-examinationrefusal-to-change

A few months ago, our office had a family event. One of the stalls was a pottery stall. My wife wanted to try it. So she sat with the potter while I stood nearby, watching and capturing those moments on my phone.

It was fascinating.

The potter placed a lump of soft clay on the wheel and showed her how to shape it into a beautiful pot.

It looked easy.

But as the wheel kept spinning...

the clay would sometimes lose its shape.

Each time, the potter gently placed his hands on it...

reshaped it...

corrected it...

and started again.

Because the clay was still soft. It could still be shaped. Then I looked at the finished pots displayed beside us. I asked the potter,

"Can these be reshaped too?"

He smiled and said,

"No."

"Once the clay becomes hard..."

"it can no longer be reshaped."

"If there's a mistake..."

"the only option is to break it."

That answer stayed with me.

🪞 The Mirror

Life can be like that. The Potter never struggles to shape clay that is still soft. Maybe the question isn't whether God is still shaping me.

Maybe the question is...

Is my heart still soft enough to be shaped? There was a time when I could accept correction.

I would listen.

Think.

Change.

But if I keep saying,

"I'm fine."

"I know."

"This is just how I am."

Slowly...

my heart begins to harden. The problem isn't that the Potter stops shaping. The problem is that I slowly stop responding.

💭 Think about it...

When was the last time I accepted a correction...

and truly changed because of it?

If the Potter placed His hands on my life today...

would He find soft clay...

or a heart that no longer wants to be shaped?
