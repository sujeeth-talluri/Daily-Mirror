The Sandcastle

self-examination

A father took his little son to the beach.

The little boy spent the whole afternoon building the biggest sandcastle he had ever made.

He carefully shaped every tower.

He even built a small wall around it.

He decorated it with tiny shells.

Every few minutes he would call out,

"Dad... come and see!"

His father smiled every single time.

As the sun began to set, the father said,

"Come on, son. It's time to go."

The little boy looked at his castle.

"But Dad..."

"The waves..."

"I know."

"They'll wash it away."

The father nodded.

"They will."

The little boy stood there quietly.

The first wave touched the little wall.

The next swallowed one of the towers.

Another erased everything else.

Within minutes, there was nothing left except smooth sand.

The little boy couldn't take his eyes off the empty beach.

After a long silence, he looked up and asked,

"Can't we stay until tomorrow?"

The father knelt beside him, smiled gently and said,

"Even if we did...

the tide would still come."

"Some things are beautiful...

but they were never meant to last."

🪞 Mirror

Funny, isn't it?

Every one of us understands why that little boy wanted just one more day.

Just one more sunrise.

Just one more chance to admire what he had built.

The truth is...

most of us aren't much different.

We've all whispered our own version of,

"Can't it stay... just a little longer?"

A season.

A relationship.

A title.

A possession.

A person.

Not because any of them were bad.

Because they were precious.

But life has a tide that never asks our permission.

It simply keeps coming.

Think about it.

What am I holding so tightly today... that one day I'll have to let go anyway?
