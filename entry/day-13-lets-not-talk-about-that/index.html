Let's Not Talk About That.

POSTPONEMENT

She was the kind of friend everyone admired. If she made a mistake, she'd always say,

"Tell me honestly... what could I have done better?"
She listened.
She learned.
She grew.
Years passed.

Life was going well. She was doing well at work.

Slowly, something changed.

She still enjoyed appreciation. But correction became harder to hear. One evening, her closest friend said,

"There's something I've been wanting to tell you. I'm worried about where you're heading."
She smiled.
"Please... let's not talk about that."
The conversation ended.

Months later, the very thing her friend wanted to warn her about had become a painful reality.

Looking back, she realized...

Her life didn't begin to change when things fell apart. It began the day she stopped welcoming the truth.

🪞 The Mirror...

Today, I could be either person. Maybe I'm the friend who has been avoiding a difficult conversation because I don't want to hurt someone I care about. Or maybe I'm the one who keeps saying,

"Let's not talk about that."

Real friendships need both. One person with the courage to lovingly speak the truth. Another with the humility to receive it.

Who am I today?
