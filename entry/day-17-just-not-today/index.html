Just Not Today.

PRIDE · FEAR · POSTPONEMENT

Before they got married, they made a promise to each other.

"No matter how big the fight is, let's never sleep without sorting it out."

They both believed it. In fact, whenever friends asked for marriage advice, they would say,

"Never let silence grow. Sit together, talk it through and sort it out."

Years later, one painful argument changed the atmosphere at home. He wanted to go and talk. But a thought came.

"Why should I go first? I didn't start this."

She wanted to talk too. But another thought came.

"If I go first, he'll think what he did was okay."

That night, neither of them spoke. The next day, both wanted to. But now it felt awkward.

Three days passed.
Then a week.

It wasn't that they no longer valued communication. They simply convinced themselves that, for this situation, staying silent was the better decision.

🪞 The Mirror...

I think this is how most of us slowly drift.

Not because we stop believing our values...

But because pressure gives us reasons that sound more convincing than the values we once held. It's easy to say,

"These are my values."
Life quietly replies,
"Show them to me."

The values I truly live by are not the ones I speak about when life is easy. They're the ones that remain when fear, hurt, pride or pressure ask me to choose differently.

The values I say I have... are they really the values my decisions reveal?
