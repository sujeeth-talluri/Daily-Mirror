The Announcement

COMPARISON · URGENCY VS TRUTH · POSTPONEMENT

A train was expected any minute. The platform was crowded.

Families.
Students.
Office-goers.

People carrying bags bigger than themselves.

Suddenly someone shouted,
"Wrong platform! The train is coming on Platform 2."

Within seconds people started running. Some picked up two bags. Some dragged their children. Some didn't even know who shouted. They simply followed everyone else. Nobody stopped to check. Only one elderly porter stayed where he was. A man running past asked,

"Why are you still sitting? Didn't you hear?"
The porter smiled.
"I heard."
"I'm just waiting for the announcement."

Less than a minute later, the station announcement came.

"The train will arrive on Platform 5."

The same platform everyone had left.

People slowly walked back...
carrying the same luggage they had hurried away with.

The porter hadn't moved an inch.

🪞 Mirror

Nobody stopped to check.

One person shouted.
Everyone reacted.
Makes me think.

Do I react to every voice I hear... or do I first stop and check if it's true?
