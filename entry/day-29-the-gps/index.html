The GPS

FALSE SECURITY · MILESTONE VS JOURNEY · POSTPONEMENT

A man was driving to a place he had never been before. He switched on the GPS.

"Turn left in 200 metres."
"I know a better shortcut," he said, driving straight ahead.
A few minutes later...
"Make a U-turn when possible."
He smiled.
"It'll recalculate."
It did.
"Turn right after 500 metres."

Again, he ignored it.

"This road is faster."

Twenty minutes later, he found himself in a narrow dead-end street. Frustrated, he stopped the car.

"This GPS is useless."

His friend, who had been sitting quietly all along, looked at him and asked,

"Can I see your phone?"

He opened the route history. Every turn the GPS suggested was still there. Every wrong turn belonged to the driver. His friend handed the phone back and smiled.

"The GPS didn't fail you."
"You stopped trusting it."

Then he asked one more question.

"If you weren't going to follow it...
why did you switch it on?"

The rest of the journey was silent.

🪞 Mirror

Funny, isn't it?

Sometimes we don't reject guidance because it is wrong. We reject it because we believe we know a better way.

We ask for direction...
only to follow our own instincts.

Then, when life becomes confusing, it's easy to blame the guidance. Sometimes the problem isn't the guidance. It's the guide we choose to believe.

Think about it.

Where in my life am I asking for direction... while quietly deciding to go my own way anyway?
