The Kite

postponement

A father was flying a kite with his young son.

The wind was strong.

The kite kept climbing higher and higher.

The little boy couldn't stop smiling.

After watching it for a while, he looked at the string and said,

"Dad... if we cut the string, it'll go even higher."

The father smiled.

He didn't answer.

A few moments later, he handed the spool to his son.

"Go ahead."

The boy carefully cut the string.

For a few seconds...

the kite shot even higher.

The boy jumped with excitement.

"See! I told you!"

His father just watched.

Within moments, the kite began to wobble.

The wind that once lifted it...

now pushed it wherever it wanted.

It drifted lower...

and lower...

until it disappeared behind a row of trees.

The boy stood there quietly.

His father gently placed a hand on his shoulder and said,

"It wasn't the string that kept it from flying."

"It was the string that kept it flying."

🪞 Mirror

Makes me think.

Have I ever mistaken the very thing that keeps me strong... as the thing that's holding me back?
