The Mentor

pridehidden-intentionsrefusal-to-changepostponement

As a young boy, he lost his parents.

A retired school teacher took him into his home.

He raised him like his own son.

He taught him to tell the truth, keep his word, respect people, and never let success change who he was.

Years later, the boy grew into a remarkable young man.

He built a successful company.

People admired not only his success, but also his character.

Whenever someone praised him, he would smile and say,

"I learnt all this from my teacher."

A few years later, the old teacher passed away.

Life became busier.

Success brought new friendships.

New advisors.

New circles.

New voices.

He didn't suddenly become a different person.

He simply started spending more time listening to different voices than the one that had built him.

Slowly, his choices changed.

Then his habits changed.

Then his character changed.

Years later, he met one of his teacher's old friends.

After a long conversation, the old man smiled and said,

"Your teacher would have been so proud to see the man you've become."

He smiled politely.

But as he drove home, one thought refused to leave him.

"Would he really?"

That question hurt.

Not because he didn't know what was right...

But because deep inside, he knew he had slowly become someone his younger self would never have wanted to become.

That stayed with me.

🪞 The Mirror...

None of us is beyond influence.

The voices we keep listening to...

The people we keep spending time with...

The ideas we keep feeding our minds...

Slowly shape the person we're becoming.

If I pause and honestly look at my life today...

Am I becoming the person I once hoped to be...

Or have I slowly drifted from the very convictions that once defined me?

Because we rarely drift in one big decision.

First, our ears change. Then, our lives do.
