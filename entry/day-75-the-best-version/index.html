The Best Version

COMPLACENCY · SELF-EXAMINATION

Today I was in Raipur as part of a campus recruitment panel, interviewing final-year students at NIT. One thing I've always observed during campus placements is how different the campus looks on interview day. Students who usually attend classes in casual wear suddenly come in their best attire.

Shoes are polished.

Hair is neatly groomed. Resumes are carefully arranged. Everything about them reflects preparation.

Why?

Because they know this opportunity matters. They want the interview panel to see the best version of themselves. As I watched them throughout the day, one thought stayed with me. Every one of us knows what our best looks like. We've all brought it out at different points in our lives.

🪞 The Mirror

Looking into my own mirror...

The more I thought about it, the more I realised something. It isn't that we don't have a better version of ourselves. We've already proved we do.

The real question is...
What do I consider important enough to bring that version out?

When something truly matters to us, preparation comes naturally.

Attention comes naturally.
Effort comes naturally.

The best version of us quietly appears. That made me pause.

Where have I slowly stopped bringing my best...
simply because it has become familiar?

Maybe our priorities are revealed less by what we say is important...
and more by where the best version of ourselves consistently shows up.

💭 Think about it...

Where has the best version of me been showing up lately? What has only been receiving whatever is left over?

What have I quietly stopped treating as important enough...
to deserve my very best?
