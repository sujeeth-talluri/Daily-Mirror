The Two Trees

SELF-EXAMINATION · FALSE SECURITY

A gardener planted two young trees on the same day. They stood only a few feet apart. Both received the same sunlight.

The same rain.
The same soil.
The same care.
Year after year...
both grew well.
Both looked green.
Both looked healthy.

Anyone passing by would have said,

"There is no difference between these two trees."
Then one year...
a long drought came.
Week after week...
there was no rain.

Slowly, one tree began to dry up.

Its leaves fell.

Its branches became empty. The other tree stood just as green as before. When the drought was over, someone asked the gardener,

"Both trees received the same care. Why did one survive and the other die?"

The gardener smiled and said,

"The drought didn't make the difference."
"It only revealed it."
"One tree had roots that had gone deep into the ground."
"The other looked healthy above the ground... but its roots never grew deep enough."

🪞 The Mirror

That made me think. Life can be like that.

When life is good...
many of us look strong.
We smile.
We work.
We celebrate.
From the outside...
it's hard to tell the difference.
Then life changes.
A disappointment.
A sickness.
A broken relationship.
Financial pressure.
A loss.

That's when the roots begin to speak.

A faith that has quietly grown deep...
holds on.
A faith that only looked strong on the outside...
struggles to stand.

The pressure doesn't make my faith weak. It reveals how deep it really is.

💭 Think about it...

If a season of pressure came into my life today...
what would it reveal about the roots of my faith?

Am I really green...
or have I only looked green while life was easy?

The drought won't decide the strength of my roots.

It will simply reveal them.
