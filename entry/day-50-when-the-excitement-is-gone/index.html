When the Excitement Is Gone

MILESTONE VS JOURNEY · PREMATURE SURRENDER

Back in 2017, I was introduced to running through a program called Couch to 5K.

Everything felt exciting.
My first 5K.

My first half marathon.

My first 25K.

Every new milestone gave me another reason to keep going.

But once the excitement slowly faded...
so did my consistency.

Little by little, I drifted back into my old ways. In 2023, I started again. This time, I didn't promise myself a marathon. I made a much smaller promise. Just show up today.

One run.
One workout.

One day at a time. Some mornings were exciting.

Many weren't.

Some days I saw progress. Many days I didn't. But I kept showing up. As I sat down to write today's Daily Mirror, I realized this is Day 50. I never started this journey thinking about writing fifty reflections.

I simply wrote one...
and came back the next day.

One day at a time.

🪞 The Mirror

It's surprisingly difficult to stay consistent. Not because it's impossible. But because it's ordinary. There is no excitement.

No applause.
No immediate results.

The people who remain consistent aren't people who wake up motivated every day. They simply refuse to walk away from the commitments they have made. One prayer at a time. One workout at a time. One page at a time. One kind decision at a time. One day at a time. Looking back, I realised something. The biggest changes in my life didn't happen because of one extraordinary day. They happened because of hundreds of ordinary days that looked like nothing was changing. People celebrate the milestones. Life is changed by the ordinary days between them.

💭 Think about it...

How many good things have I started...
only to walk away when they stopped being exciting?

How many promises have I made to myself...
that are still waiting for me to simply show up again today?

What if the life I'm praying for...
isn't waiting for one extraordinary moment...
but for my decision to remain faithful...

one day at a time?
