The Dusty Recipe Book

POSTPONEMENT

A young woman wanted to recreate her late mother's famous biryani. She remembered the aroma.

The taste.

The joy it brought to every family gathering.

She searched everywhere.

Watched countless cooking videos.

Tried different recipes.
Asked relatives.
Still...

it never tasted the same. One day, while cleaning an old kitchen cupboard, she noticed a dusty notebook tucked away at the back.

She opened it.

Every recipe was there. Written carefully in her mother's own handwriting. She stood there quietly, holding it for a long time. The recipe had never been lost. It had simply been forgotten.

She had spent months searching everywhere else...

while the answer had been waiting in her own kitchen.

🪞 The Mirror

That made me think. Sometimes I do the same.

When life becomes confusing...

I start searching everywhere for answers.

New ideas.
New voices.
New solutions.

Yet I quietly neglect the truth that has been with me all along. The problem isn't always that I don't have direction.

Sometimes...

I've simply stopped opening it.

💭 Think about it...

What answer am I searching everywhere else for...
when it has been waiting patiently for me all along?

Have I neglected what I already know...
while chasing something new?

Maybe the answer I'm looking for...

isn't waiting somewhere else.

Maybe it's waiting for me to open it again.
