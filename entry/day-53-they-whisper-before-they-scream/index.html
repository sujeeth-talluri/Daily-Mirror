They Whisper Before They Scream

IGNORING WARNING SIGNS · COMPLACENCY

A few days ago, my wife woke up in the middle of the night with a severe toothache after having an ice cream. The pain stayed until noon the next day. By evening, it had settled down. It looked like everything was back to normal. That evening, she had a chilled soft drink.

Later that night...
another ice cream.

The next morning, she woke up with a sore throat. Then came the fever. Instead of going to college, she spent the day resting at home. Looking back, the fever wasn't where it all started. The body had already been giving its warnings. The fever was simply what happened when those warnings were ignored.

🪞 The Mirror

That incident stayed with me. Looking into my own mirror, I realised something. Life rarely falls apart overnight.

More often than not, it whispers...
before it screams.

One of the saddest things about leprosy isn't the wounds. It's that the body slowly loses its ability to feel pain.

The very warning God designed to protect the body...
is no longer there.

That made me wonder.

How many warnings has God lovingly placed in my life...
not to disturb me...
but to protect me?

Sometimes it's a conversation I know I need to have. Sometimes it's a habit I know I need to stop. Sometimes it's my health quietly asking me to slow down. Sometimes it's that gentle conviction in my heart that refuses to go away. Maybe God's warnings aren't interruptions. Maybe they're His compassion. Because the biggest failures in life are rarely one bad decision.

They're usually the result of many small warnings...
quietly ignored.

💭 Think about it...

What warning in my life have I become so familiar with...
that I've stopped listening to it?

What if the whisper I'm trying to ignore today...

is actually God's way of protecting me from tomorrow's pain?
