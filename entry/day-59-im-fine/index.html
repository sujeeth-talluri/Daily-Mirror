I'm Fine.

FALSE PEACE · GRIEF

A few years ago, life took me through a season I never imagined I would have to walk through.

People would ask,
"How are you?"

Without even thinking, I would smile and say,

"I'm fine."

The conversation would end. But the grief didn't. For a long time, I thought that's what being strong looked like.

Carry on.
Keep yourself busy.

Don't let people see your pain. Don't talk about it. Don't cry about it.

Just move on.
But the more I tried to bury my grief...
the longer it stayed buried within me.

Pretending didn't make me stronger. It only delayed my healing.

Everything slowly changed the day I stopped fighting my grief...
and started walking through it.

Looking back today, I realise something. My running journey wasn't just about becoming a better runner. These Daily Mirrors weren't just about becoming a better writer.

Without even planning it...
both became part of my healing.

Then I began noticing something beautiful.

Some of the greatest songs ever written...
the most moving books ever published...
the most breathtaking paintings ever created...
and some of the strongest people I've ever met...
all have one thing in common.
Somewhere behind them...
there's a grief story.

Grief has a strange way of changing us.

It either remains buried within us...
or it quietly becomes the birthplace of something beautiful.

🪞 The Mirror

It's strange.

When we're happy, we never hide it.

We laugh.
We celebrate.

We share it with everyone around us.

But when we're hurting...
we somehow feel the need to hide.
Almost as though joy is acceptable...
but grief isn't.

Yet both are part of being human. Both were placed within us.

Looking into my own mirror...

I wonder how many of us have become so good at saying,

"I'm fine."

that we've forgotten how to say,

"I'm hurting."
Perhaps we've mistaken pretending for strength...
when all along...
honesty was the first step towards healing.

💭 Think about it...

How do I respond when life breaks me?

Do I hide my grief...
or do I allow it to find a voice?

Have I been delaying my healing...
simply because I've been hiding what hurts?

What beautiful thing might be waiting to be born...

if I finally stop pretending that I'm fine?
