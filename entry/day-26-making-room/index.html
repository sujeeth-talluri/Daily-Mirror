Making Room

POSTPONEMENT

A man finally bought the car he had been saving for. He was excited to park it inside his garage. He opened the garage door.

Then he stopped.

The garage was full.

Old paint cans.
Broken chairs.
Empty cardboard boxes.

A bicycle no one had ridden for years. His son looked inside and asked,

"Dad... where are we going to keep the car?"
The father smiled.
"First...
we make room."
"But what if we need some of these things later?"
"We might."
"But if we keep everything...
there won't be room for what matters today."

So they spent the entire day clearing the garage. By evening, it looked almost empty. The son looked around and said,

"It feels strange."
The father nodded.
"It always does."

Then he slowly drove the new car inside.

For the first time...

the garage was doing what it was built for.

🪞 Mirror

Funny, isn't it?

We all love new beginnings.

A new season.
A new opportunity.
A new relationship.
A fresh start.
But many times...

we step into something new while dragging yesterday along with us. Old disappointments into new opportunities. Old hurts into new relationships. Old failures into new dreams. Then we wonder why the new never feels truly new.

Sometimes the hardest part of a new beginning...

is letting an old ending stay where it belongs. Not because the past had no value.

But because if we keep giving it space...

there may be no room left for what today is trying to build.

Think about it.

What am I still holding on to from yesterday... that's leaving no room for what today is trying to build?
